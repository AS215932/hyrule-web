What “no KYC” means here

Hyrule Cloud does not ask for government ID, a phone number or a card. One-shot orders need no account at all. Optional accounts use a generated H<10 hex> handle with a recovery code or wallet-signature recovery.

No KYC does not mean no rules. Payment and lifecycle records are kept as described in the privacy policy, and the terms and abuse policy apply. The network is AS215932, operated from the Netherlands.
